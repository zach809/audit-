What changed:
- Added an optional AI Diagnostics Assistant in the Reports tab.
- AI Diagnostics summarizes saved Audit Logic Issues and explains likely causes in plain English.
- AI Diagnostics can call out patterns like Clio API/read errors, evidence wording mismatches, unclear communication direction, stale rows needing recheck, date-window issues, or rules that may need tuning.
- AI Diagnostics provides recommended checks, rule-tuning ideas, Clio setup checks, and a copyable diagnostics note.
- AI Diagnostics uses saved audit metadata only; it does not send communication bodies, note text, document contents, billing data, or payment data.
- AI Diagnostics does not change Clio, does not change audit results, and does not repair logic automatically.
- Added an optional AI Review Assistant inside the weekly review builder.
- The AI button drafts Results Details, Report Summary, and a Teams message for the selected flagged matter.
- AI drafts are not saved automatically; the auditor must review and save them.
- The AI helper does not write to Clio, does not change audit decisions, and does not mark items resolved.
- The AI helper sends only selected audit metadata and auditor-entered notes to OpenAI for drafting.
- The AI helper does not send communication bodies, note text, document contents, billing data, or payment data.
- If OPENAI_API_KEY is missing, CWCA still works normally and shows an AI-not-configured message.

- Broadened weekly client check-in matching for weekly follow-up, weekly client follow-up, weekly check-in, weekly call, and courtesy call wording.
- Calendar scanning now looks from 30 days before matter creation through 365 days ahead, so future or slightly early linked events are less likely to be missed.
