What changed:
- Fixed communication date handling so CWCA uses the most precise Clio timestamp available.

- The same timestamp fix also helps Appearance Filed, Client Contact, Court Results, and other communication-based proof checks.
- Evidence links in matter cards now say "Open Proof in Clio" so auditors know the link goes straight to Clio for confirmation.
- The manual AI chat now receives the saved audit row details, including reason code, operational state, proof reference, audit version, and last checked time.
- When asked what is wrong or how to improve a rule, AI should now explain the actual saved CWCA issue instead of repeating generic guidance.
- Made the matter AI helper a small chat box by default.
- Removed the empty AI answer panel before a question is asked.
- Moved quick prompts into a Suggested questions dropdown.
- The main visible control is now a compact one-line question box with an Ask button.
- Changed the matter AI helper into a direct mini chat for each flagged item.
- Auditors can now type their own question about a flag instead of only receiving a fixed AI summary.
- Added quick buttons for: Where did CWCA look, Could this be a false positive, What proof should I check, and How can we improve this rule.
- The chat is still manual-only and only uses the selected matter/audit item metadata.
- The chat is meant to help debug CWCA logic and understand possible app false positives.
- Improved the manual AI helper so it gives more useful auditor guidance instead of generic "check Clio" wording.
- The AI helper now receives the specific workflow rule, goal, expected proof type, and suggested Clio area for the selected item.
- AI output now includes: expected proof, where to check, why it was flagged, recommended auditor action, possible CWCA issue, and a copy-ready team note.
- The AI helper includes an Open Clio shortcut that points to the most relevant Clio area for that issue.
- Reverted the AI helper back to the OpenAI setup.
- Polished the matter-level AI helper panel so it is shorter, easier to scan, and less system-looking.
- The AI result now shows one Summary, a short checklist of what to check, and a collapsed copy-ready team note.
- The copy button now says Copy Note instead of Copy AI Note.
- Added a manual AI chat box on individual matter problem cards.
- AI now runs only when a user clicks that specific issue button or the existing Draft with AI button in the weekly review builder.
- Removed the broad AI Diagnostics panel from the visible Reports tab so AI does not feel like an automatic/bulk analyzer.
- The new manual AI helper sends only selected matter/audit-item metadata to OpenAI.
- It does not send communication bodies, note text, document contents, billing data, or payment data.
- The helper explains the selected issue in plain English, suggests what to check in Clio, flags possible CWCA matching/rule issues, and gives a copyable team note.
- AI does not write to Clio, does not mark anything resolved, and does not change audit results.
- The app remains read-only against Clio.
